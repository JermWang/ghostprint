    What a photo carries

    One photo.
    Four ways to find you.

    Phones and cameras write a hidden record into every file. Social apps often strip it, but email attachments, cloud links, forums and marketplace listings usually pass it along untouched.

    Where you stood

    GPSLatitude 40.71280° N

    Phones record coordinates to within a few meters. A photo taken on your porch carries your address.

    What you carry

    Model iPhone 15 Pro

    Make, model and lens narrow down who took a photo, and they repeat on every shot from the same phone.

    When you were there

    DateTimeOriginal 18:22:07 −04:00

    A timestamp with a time-zone offset places you in a region and an hour, even with location turned off.

    Which device, exactly

    BodySerialNumber 052021003418

    Serial numbers and unique image IDs link photos posted from different accounts back to one camera.

    Nothing uploads

    Your photo is read and rewritten inside this tab. Close it and nothing is left anywhere.

    Lossless when it can be

    For JPEG, PNG and WebP, only the metadata blocks are cut. The image data is copied byte for byte.

    Rotation stays right

    If a photo depends on its orientation tag, Ghostprint bakes the rotation into the pixels first.

    Colors stay true

    Color profiles are kept so skin tones and skies don't shift. They describe the screen, not you.

    How it works

    Three steps, all in this tab.

    A photo file is a stack of labeled blocks. Ghostprint reads each block's label, decodes the ones that describe you, and writes a new file without them.
