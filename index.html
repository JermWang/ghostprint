>>>Ghost mode tears out the pages.

Fresh wallet per buy

Each ghost buy runs from a new wallet with no history. Trackers see a stranger every time.

Private routing

Funds reach the ghost through NEAR Intents' Confidential Intents and are paid out by NEAR's bridge, not by you. We don't run a mixer.

Rebuild anywhere

Ghost wallets come from one signature of your wallet. Sign again on any device to get them back. Nothing secret is stored.

No fee trail

The fee comes out of your main wallet when you fund. Ghosts never pay Ghostprint, so nothing on-chain marks them as ours.

How Ghost mode uses NEAR Intents

  1. Ask for a route. The terminal asks NEAR Intents' 1Click API to deliver SOL to a brand-new ghost wallet. 1Click answers with a one-time deposit address.
  2. Fund it once. You sign a single transfer from your wallet into that deposit address. Ghostprint's 0.5% fee rides in the same transaction.
  3. Solvers pay the ghost. NEAR Intents fills the intent from its own liquidity and pays the ghost wallet. With Confidential Intents, the route's details stay off the public record.
  4. The ghost buys. Once the SOL lands, the ghost swaps through Jupiter. Exits run the same route in reverse, back to your wallet.
